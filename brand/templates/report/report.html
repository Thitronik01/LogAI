Detection limits

These exports describe network activity during the stated hour. They cannot establish which person used a service or what a request contained. Byte counters can include protocol overhead.

Visibility depends on the log source, the captured hostname and the catalogue snapshot. An unmatched record may concern an unknown service. The policy label describes the organisation’s list for that service. It does not determine the purpose of a connection.
